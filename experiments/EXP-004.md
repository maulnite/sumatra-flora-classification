## Findings
- Test is harder than validation: fold-0 validation 0.651 vs public 0.519.
- Hardest classes: Gymnocalycium, Anacampseros, Lagerstroemia, Ixora.
- Dendrobium leonis and Cuphea were predicted about 1.5x more often than in train.

## Limitations
- Validation corruption has no rotation; test images are rotated uniformly over 360 degrees (found later).
- Public score comes from one fold model only.
