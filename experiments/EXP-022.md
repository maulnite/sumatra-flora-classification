## Hypothesis
Macro F1 rewards balanced predictions; shifting class weights so the mean prediction matches the train prior should help without labels.

| Key | Value |
| --- | --- |

## Findings

- Public 0.82164, +0.015 over the 224 ensemble (0.80694).

## Limitations
