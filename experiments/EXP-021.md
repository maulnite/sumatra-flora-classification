## Findings
- Colour and contrast normalisation hurts (-0.05 to -0.13): the model needs colour cues.
- Autocrop of black borders slightly hurts (-0.013).
- Inference resolution 288-320 gives +0.03 to +0.04 for these 224-trained models (FixRes-style effect).

## Limitations
- Member models were trained at 224; the later EXP-018/020 results show a larger effect when training resolution matches.
